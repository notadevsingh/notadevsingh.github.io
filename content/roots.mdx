I never learned to accept "this is just how it is." Every belief, every
assumption — I had to pull it apart, find a reason, and build my own story
around it. Sometimes that story held up. Sometimes it didn't. Either way, the
habit stuck.

That same restlessness found its natural home in technology. Computers didn't
just answer questions — they let me ask better ones. I studied Computer Science
and Engineering at Amity University, graduating in 2011, but the real education
was the instinct that got me there: an itch to understand _why_ before
accepting _what_.

Everything on this site — the products I've shaped, the side-projects I've
built, the things I've taught — grew out of that one reflex: don't just look
at it, take it apart.
