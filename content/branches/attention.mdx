Sitting with something until you see what is actually there, rather than what
you assumed was there. It started on paper and it never stayed there. The same
instinct that made me redraw a line until it was right is the one that makes me
rewrite a spec until the edge cases stop hiding.

This is the branch that connects everything else. The questioning from the
roots, the checking from practice, the documenting from teaching — all of it is
just this habit applied in different rooms.
