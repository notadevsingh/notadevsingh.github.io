Planning a two-week ride and planning a delivery are closer than they sound.
Both are mostly about what you decide _not_ to carry.

Bombay to Hyderabad : Hyderabad to Delhi
Solo on bike. Chandigarh to Leh and Ladakh over ten days. Backpacking across Europe.
Each trip taught logistics, risk, and the difference between a plan and a route.

Somewhere along the way this turned into Happy Horizons — a small
community-travel project run through Instagram, and a trekking side business
that lives alongside the day job.
