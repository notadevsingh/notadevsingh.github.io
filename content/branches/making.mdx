These are here because the pattern they show — taking on the whole job,
including the parts nobody assigned — is the same one running through the
professional branches. An app on the Play Store, scripts that solved problems
before anyone filed a ticket, and whatever seemed interesting enough to finish
on a weekend.
In the age of AI, everyone can build. The difference is knowing _what_ to build
and _why_ it matters. That judgement is what this branch is really about.
