Manual QA to Automation Engineer to Test Lead to Business Analyst to Product
Owner. The route in matters more than the title. Coming up through the checking
side means the failure modes arrive _before_ the plan does — some people read
that as pessimism, I read it as the most useful instinct I carry.

Every role change was really the same skill applied at a wider angle: first I
checked code, then I checked systems, then I checked whether the system was
worth building in the first place.
