This started before any job did — tutoring school kids, deciphering CS subjects
for classmates. At Accenture it turned into grooming four or five junior
resources. After that it became internal wikis, training sessions across teams,
self-teaching Selenium and AutoHotkey and then passing those skills on before
anyone made it a process.

None of this was ever a designation, which is how I know it is real rather than
assigned. The instinct is always the same: if I had to figure it out the hard
way, the next person shouldn't have to.
