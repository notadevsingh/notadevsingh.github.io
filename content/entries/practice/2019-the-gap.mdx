## What it was

Two or three sentences, and at least one of them carries a year, a place, or
a number. Name what the organisation did in words a stranger would use.
"A logistics firm" is a category anyone could type; "a firm that moved 40
trucks of produce a night between two cities" is a fact only you would know.

## What I did

The specific calls *you* made, not the team's. Where you were wrong first is
more interesting than where you were right. Dates and names belong here: the
month it turned, the person who disagreed, the document that settled it.

## What changed

The outcome, in whatever unit is honest: a number, a date it stopped hurting,
a thing the team does differently now. A claim with no unit is a claim anyone
could make, so the report warns when a note has none.

## What I'd redo

One thing. The build will not let you skip it, including on the wins. A note
with no regret in it is a note nobody believes.
