---
year: 2021
title: Writing it down so nobody has to ask twice
summary: Stopped answering the same question in person and started leaving a document where the question would be asked.
expanded: >
  Whenever I watched a team rediscover the same fix from scratch, I wrote
  it up. Not polished docs — just clear enough that the next person didn't
  need to tap anyone on the shoulder. Over time those pages became the ones
  people actually opened.
where: Product & Engineering
