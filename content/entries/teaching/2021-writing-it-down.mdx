## What it was

A team of 14 that answered the same three questions every week, from a
different person each time, starting in the spring of 2019.

## What I did

Wrote the answers down where the questions were asked, in the Tuesday
stand-up notes, and then kept doing it until asking became rarer than reading.

## What changed

The questions did not stop. They changed, which is the only outcome worth
having. By 2021 the log had 60 entries and two of them were mine.

## What I'd redo

Started two years earlier, before the questions felt like a problem.
